## 📌 Project Overview

**AgentCraft Studio** is a conceptual agency brand and digital marketing capstone project engineered to bridge technical software engineering with modern digital service marketing. 

The project addresses a common gap in small-to-medium business digital marketing: **visitor lead drop-off due to slow response times**. While traditional marketing drives traffic to static landing pages, AgentCraft Studio introduces a conversion-first agency model that bundles high-speed web design with 24/7 on-page AI chatbots and automated lead pipelines.

---

## 🎯 Digital Marketing Strategy & Positioning

### 1. Value Proposition (USP)
* *"Websites & Smart AI Bots Made Simple"*
* Eliminates technical intimidation for non-technical small business owners.
* Positions AI not as a complex enterprise experiment, but as an affordable daily automation utility that saves 2+ hours daily and answers visitor inquiries in under 5 minutes.

### 2. Target Market
* Local retail shops, service providers, clinics, law chambers, and boutique consultants.
* Solopreneurs and digital creators seeking high-conversion landing pages.

### 3. Inbound Funnel Design
* **Top of Funnel (Awareness):** Educational carousel posts comparing static chatbots vs. action-oriented AI agents, prompt engineering tips, and page-speed comparisons.
* **Middle of Funnel (Consideration):** Live interactive bot playground on the agency website allowing prospects to test real-time intent classification before buying.
* **Bottom of Funnel (Decision/Conversion):** Real-time interactive pricing and scope calculator that auto-populates the discovery consultation form with zero friction.

## 💻 Technical Stack & Implementation

The website is engineered as a single-page, high-performance web asset prioritizing Core Web Vitals and SEO:

* **Markup & Styling:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS, FontAwesome 6, Google Fonts (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Space Grotesk`, `JetBrains Mono`).
* **Client-Side Scripting:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) for zero external dependency overhead and instant loading speeds.
* **Interactive AI Simulation:** 
  * In-browser intent evaluation pipeline replicating model API endpoints.
  * Dual-view mode (Human conversational view vs. Structured JSON payload view).
  * Strict prompt guardrails simulation to showcase hallucination prevention.
* **Dynamic Cost Calculator:** Real-time client scope estimator updating package tiers, add-on pricing, and auto-injecting selections into the contact form.
* **Theme Management:** System-aware and manual Dark/Light mode toggle with `localStorage` state persistence.
